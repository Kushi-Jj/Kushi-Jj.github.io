Overview

My research combines physiological, behavioral, and machine learning approaches to enhance human performance and safety in extreme environments.

We analyze multimodal data — brain imaging, kinematics, eye gaze, muscle activation, and heart rate — to understand human sensorimotor function.

The goal is to design human–machine systems that jointly optimize performance, system effectiveness, and safety.
