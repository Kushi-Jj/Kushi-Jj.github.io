Research Overview

Analyzing multimodal physiological and behavioral data to understand human sensorimotor control under extreme conditions like space travel and firefighting.

Integrating insights from brain imaging, movement kinematics, eye gaze, muscle activation, heart rate, and musculoskeletal analysis using statistics, machine learning, and deep learning–based temporal models.

Translating insights into human–machine system design and control to optimize human performance, system efficiency, and safety.

Spaceflight Human Optimization

Multiple Spaceflight Hazards

Study of how altered gravity, fatigue, and high workload affect astronaut sensorimotor performance and adaptation.

Data Collection & Synchronization

Multimodal streams synchronized with Lab Streaming Layer (LSL) for high-fidelity human performance analysis.

Simulating Altered Gravity

Using Galvanic Vestibular Stimulation (GVS) to perturb vestibular signals and study balance and sensorimotor responses.

Sensorimotor Test Battery

13-task battery to assess combined effects of GVS and fatigue on performance.

Fatigue Assessment

Mental fatigue induced with two-back task; sleep-deprivation tested overnight. Measured using PVT, KSS, and POMS.

Assistive Technologies

Temporal Convolutional Networks (TCN) predict gait instability for proactive interventions.

Firefighter Assistive Devices

Developing exoskeletons to reduce injuries and enhance firefighter safety during high-load tasks.

Closed-Loop Control

PID controllers regulate muscle activation for improved comfort and safety.

Hardware & Control Architecture

Actuators selected based on biomechanical demands; communication via CAN bus.

Fire Safety in Coal Yards

Proactive methods to mitigate spontaneous ignition in coal yards. Related publications available.
